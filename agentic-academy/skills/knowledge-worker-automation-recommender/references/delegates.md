# Specialist Delegates (Subagents)

A delegate is a focused helper that sees only what it needs. It is useful when work gets big enough that the main chat loses the thread, or when a task needs fresh eyes that have not seen the rest of the conversation.

A delegate lives at `.claude/agents/<name>.md`.

**When to recommend these**: once the person has skills and at least one workflow, and is hitting work too big for a single chat. This is Week 7 in the course. Do not recommend a delegate to someone who has not built a project home base yet.

## The two questions before recommending a delegate

1. **Does this work overwhelm the main chat, or does it just need fresh eyes?** If yes, a delegate helps. If no, a skill is enough.
2. **Could parts run at the same time?** If several research angles or summaries are independent, parallel delegates plus one that combines them is the move. If each step needs the one before it, keep it sequential.

## Common delegates by archetype

### Content Creator
| The work | Delegate to recommend |
|---|---|
| Drafts that drift out of their voice | A voice-check delegate that reads the finished draft with fresh eyes and flags tone drift |
| Turning one article into many platforms | Parallel delegates for LinkedIn, X, and newsletter versions, run at once |

### Coach / Consultant
| The work | Delegate to recommend |
|---|---|
| Prepping for a client across several angles | Parallel delegates for the client's recent activity, their industry, and their competitors, then one that combines into a prep brief |
| Reviewing a proposal or deliverable | A review delegate that scores the draft against their quality bar with fresh eyes |

### Entrepreneur
| The work | Delegate to recommend |
|---|---|
| Research that spans market, customers, and competitors | Parallel research delegates, then one that combines into a go or no-go view |
| Checking messaging against the market | A positioning-review delegate that reads with fresh context |

### Knowledge Worker
| The work | Delegate to recommend |
|---|---|
| Three project updates that become one report | Parallel summarizers, then one that writes the combined status report and flags cross-project dependencies |
| Raw notes into a structured summary | A meeting-synthesis delegate that turns notes into decisions, owners, and next actions |

## The part people skip: the combine step

When delegates run in parallel, something has to pull the pieces together. Always recommend the combine step alongside parallel delegates. Without it, the person gets three separate outputs and has to stitch them by hand, which defeats the point.

## Recommending in plain language

Say what the delegate takes off their plate. "A helper that reviews your proposal against your own quality bar before you send it, with fresh eyes that did not write it" lands. "A review subagent" does not. The word "subagent" goes in parentheses, once.
