# Skill Bundles (Plugins / Skill Library)

A bundle is a ready-made set of skills the person installs in one move, instead of building each by hand. For someone early in their Claude Code journey, this is the single highest-leverage recommendation: ten working skills on day one, tailored to the kind of work they actually do.

**When to recommend these**: especially when the person is just starting, or has a project home base but no skills yet. This is the Week 5 install in the course.

## The four archetype Skill Libraries

Match the person's archetype to the bundle. Each library is curated for that work type, so they get firepower without sorting through generic skills.

### Content Creator Skill Library
For newsletter writers, bloggers, podcasters, video creators, and social writers.

Covers: drafting from voice and archive, editing for voice and structure, repurposing across platforms, idea expansion from notes, headline and hook generation, research synthesis to brief, social post creation, content calendar planning, archive search, voice and tone-drift check.

### Coach / Consultant Skill Library
For solo consultants, coaches, agency owners, and advisors.

Covers: client meeting prep brief, proposal drafting from past wins, meeting summary to action items, client follow-up email, case study and testimonial extraction, discovery call synthesis, deliverable formatting to standards, client research, onboarding doc creation, proposal and deliverable review.

### Entrepreneur Skill Library
For founders, product managers, marketers, and operators.

Covers: market research synthesis, customer interview to insight, positioning and messaging draft, competitor analysis, founder content, pitch and narrative refinement, customer-language extraction for copy, strategic decision brief, decision memo with evidence, OKR and priority drafting.

### Knowledge Worker Skill Library
For analysts, researchers, project managers, and team leads.

Covers: meeting notes to structured summary, status report from project files, research brief from multiple sources, stakeholder update by audience, decision memo with options and tradeoffs, cross-project synthesis, executive summary, action-item and owner extraction, cross-team report consolidation, weekly review.

## Other useful bundles

Beyond the archetype libraries, these general bundles can help depending on the work:

| If the person needs to... | Bundle direction |
|---|---|
| Make documents (Word, slides, PDFs, spreadsheets) | Document skills (docx, pptx, pdf, xlsx) |
| Do real research with citations | Research and web tooling |
| Produce visuals or infographics | Visual and design skills |

Use web search to find the current best bundle for the person's specific need if it is not one of the above.

## Recommending in plain language

Lead with the payoff. "Install the Content Creator library and you get ten skills today: drafting, editing, repurposing, and more, all built for the way creators work" lands. "Install a plugin" does not. The words "plugin" and "Skill Library" go in parentheses, once, so they learn the term the course uses.

## After they install

Tell them the next move: pick the one skill they will use most this week and run it on real work. A bundle that sits installed and unused is not progress. The win comes from using one skill on a real task and feeling the time saved.
